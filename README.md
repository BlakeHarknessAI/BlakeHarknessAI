## Background

Mechatronic engineering graduate from Massey University. Previously an engineer at MainPower, and an embedded software engineer at Levno. That engineering background is why I treat AI as a systems problem rather than a marketing one.

## What I build

Most of my work is Claude-based automation and internal tooling for New Zealand businesses:

- **Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers** that give Claude read-only access to business systems, including accounting (Xero), banking (Akahu), warehouse management (CartonCloud) and job management (SimPro).
- **Internal SaaS and automation** such as quote review, invoice extraction, document processing and reporting pipelines.
- **AI adoption programmes** including workshops and training that get non-technical teams using AI day to day.

Default stack: Next.js, Supabase, Vercel, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS. Primary tool: Claude Code.

Most client work lives in private repositories under [@Harkness-AI](https://github.com/Harkness-AI).
